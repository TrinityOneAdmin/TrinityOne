About us

St Aidan’s has stood at the top of the green since the 13th century, and the congregation that meets here now is a good deal friendlier than the building looks from the road. We keep the usual pattern of a parish week — a Sunday service, a midweek gathering for prayer, and a run of smaller groups through the week for whoever wants them.

Visiting us

There’s parking behind the hall, and the north door is the one that’s actually open. Tea and coffee after the Sunday service; nobody will make you stand up and introduce yourself.
