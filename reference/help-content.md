# TrinityOne — Help content (source copy)

Production copy for the Help/Setup guides, written for **elderly, non-technical** readers.
Same content is used in two places: **embedded on the landing page** and an **in-app Help**
section. Claude Design lays this out (see `brief-help-docs.md`). Keep wording plain; this file
is the source of truth for *what it says* — designers may adjust headings/visual grouping.

**Writing rules followed here:** short sentences, no jargon (or jargon explained once), warm
and reassuring tone, one idea per chunk, concrete analogies, clear Do / Don't.

---

## 1. Welcome — how TrinityOne keeps you safe and private

TrinityOne is different from most apps, in a good way:

- **There's no account, no password, and no email to sign up.** Nothing to forget.
- **Your phone holds a private "key" that is you.** It's created for you automatically and
  stays on your phone. It's how your church knows it's really you posting.
- **You stay anonymous unless you choose a name.** No phone number, no real name required.
  You can pick a friendly name your church will see — or stay as "Anonymous".
- **No company in the middle.** Not even we can read into your account or reset it for you.

That last point is wonderful for privacy — but it means **one thing is now your
responsibility: a short backup.** The next page is the most important one. Please read it.

---

## 2. Your 12 words — the most important thing ⭐

When you set up, TrinityOne shows you **12 little words** (your "recovery phrase").

**Think of them like the only key to a safe.** As long as you have these 12 words, you can
always get back into your account — even on a new phone. If you ever lose your phone *and*
you don't have these words written down, **no one can get the account back for you.** There's
no "forgot password" button, because there's no password and no company holding a copy.

### What to do — once
1. When the app shows your 12 words, **write them on paper**, in order, by hand.
2. Keep the paper somewhere safe at home — with important documents, or a safe.
3. (Optional) Write a second copy and keep it in a different safe place.
4. Tick **"I've saved it"** in the app.

### Please don't
- ❌ Don't take a photo or screenshot of them.
- ❌ Don't type them into a text, email, or any website.
- ❌ Don't tell anyone the words — not even someone claiming to be from the church or support.

> **Simple rule:** the 12 words live on **paper**, not on a screen, and only **you** have them.

## 3. Setting up your name and picture

You can be recognised in chat without giving any personal details.

- **Name:** tap your circle at the top, choose **Display name**, and type a friendly name —
  e.g. *"Maria from Tuesday group"*. Leave it blank to stay **Anonymous**.
- **Picture:** choose a **colour with your initial**, or pick a small **picture** from the
  gallery. (You can't upload a photo — that keeps everyone private.)

You can change these any time. They're the only things others see — never your phone number,
email, or real name.

---

## 4. Getting a new phone (or if you lose this one)

This is exactly why you wrote down your 12 words.

1. Install TrinityOne on the new phone.
2. On the welcome screen, choose **"Restore"** (or open the identity screen → **Restore**).
3. Type your 12 words in order.
4. You're back — same name, same groups.

**If you don't have the 12 words** and the old phone is gone or wiped, the old account can't
be recovered, and you'd simply start fresh with a new one. So keep that paper safe.

---

## 5. Staying safe from scams

Because you're in charge of your own key, please remember:

- **No one will ever need your 12 words.** The church won't ask. We won't ask. Support won't
  ask. **Anyone who asks for them is trying to take your account** (and any money in it).
- We will never message you asking you to "verify" or "confirm" your words or key.
- If something feels off, stop and ask a trusted person at church before doing anything.

> If in doubt: **don't share, don't type them in, just close the app and ask someone you trust.**

## 6. The giving wallet *(coming soon)*

TrinityOne will let you give to your church straight from the app, using Lightning (a fast,
low-fee way to send small amounts).

- Your wallet uses the **same 12 words** as your account — so backing them up protects your
  giving too.
- **Keep only a little in the in-app wallet** — like cash in a purse, not your savings.
- Giving can be **anonymous**: your church sees the gift, not your name, if you choose.
- You'll always see a **confirmation** that a gift went through.

(We'll add full step-by-step giving help when this feature arrives.)

## 7. Getting set up with help from a steward

If setting this up yourself feels daunting, you don't have to do it alone.

A leader or steward at your church can create an identity for you and hand it over with a
simple **QR code** (a square barcode). You just:
1. Open TrinityOne → **Restore** / **Scan invite**.
2. Point your camera at the QR code they show you.
3. That's it — you're in, no typing.

They can also help you **write down your 12 words** so your account is safely backed up from
day one. Ask anyone at church — they'll be glad to help.
