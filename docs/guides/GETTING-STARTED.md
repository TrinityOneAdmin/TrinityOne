# Getting started with TrinityOne

TrinityOne is your church's own app — Bible, chat, prayer, serving, giving — running on infrastructure the church owns, not a platform it rents. There's **no account, no email, no password**. Your identity is a key that lives on your device.

There are two ways in. Pick yours:

- **[Joining your church](#im-joining-my-church)** — someone sent you a link or a QR code.
- **[Setting up your church](#im-setting-up-my-church)** — you're the pastor/leader (a "steward").

---

## I'm joining my church

**1. Get the app.** Install the Android app, or just open the link your church sent you in a browser (it works as a web app too — you can "Add to Home Screen").

**2. First launch walks you through it.** A short setup wizard appears the first time:
- **Pick a name.** Real names are encouraged — it's a church, and it's how people recognise you. (You *can* stay anonymous, but a name helps.)
- **Write down your 12 recovery words.** ⚠️ **This is the one thing you must not skip.** There's no "forgot password" — these 12 words *are* your account. If you lose your phone and don't have them, your identity is gone. Write them on paper, keep them somewhere safe. Don't screenshot them into a cloud photo library.

**3. Follow your church.** Scan the QR or open the invite link and tap **Follow**. That's it — you'll see your church's groups, prayer, plans and announcements.

**That's the whole thing.** Explore the tabs: Read (offline Bible), Today, Community (chat + prayer), Listen/Watch.

> **Under 18, or a parent?** Child accounts are set up by a parent, with your church's steward confirming the link. See your steward — they'll walk you through it.

---

## I'm setting up my church

You'll be the **steward** — the person who runs the church side. Budget ~15 minutes.

**1. Get the Steward app** (a separate install from the member app), or open `steward.html` in a browser.

**2. Create your church key.**
- The app makes you a key and shows **12 recovery words**. ⚠️ **Back these up properly** — on paper, ideally in more than one safe place. This key *is* your church. Lose it with no backup and you can't post as the church or manage it, ever. (Optionally set a **PIN** to lock the key on the device.)
- Set your **church name** and a short description.

**3. Invite your people.**
- Tap **Invite code** — you get a shareable **link and QR**. Send it round (WhatsApp, print it in the bulletin, show the QR from the front on a Sunday).
- Members follow the link, pick a name, and they're in.

**4. Set up the basics** (all optional, do what you need):
- **Groups** — small groups, ministries, a whole-church broadcast channel. Mark youth groups **child-safe**.
- **Resources** — reading plans, devotionals, and **self-hosted sermons** (upload your own audio/video, members-only — see [Relay & hosting](RELAY-SETUP.md)).
- **Rota / Calendar / Rooms** — serving teams, run-sheets, events, room bookings.
- **Members** — see who's joined; mark children; confirm parent↔child links.
- **Finance** (optional) — a simple double-entry book with fund tracking and CSV import.

**5. Where your church lives: the relay.** By default you're on the shared TrinityOne relay and there's nothing to do. When you're ready to run your *own* — for full independence — see **[Connecting your church to a relay](RELAY-SETUP.md)**.

---

## The one rule

**Back up your recovery words.** Everything else is recoverable or optional. That isn't. → If you're worried you didn't, the app has a "back up now" prompt under Settings/Help — do it today.
